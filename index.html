Class size you can afford vs. class size you fill

A stock-and-flow planning model for an English center. Active students are a stock that flows in (acquisition) and out (graduation); the number of classes is derived from that stock and drives part-time teacher cost and room use. Every total derives from the stock — not a single intake.
